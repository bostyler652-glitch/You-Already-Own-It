# Marketing

Positioning:
"Send me what you want to buy. I'll tell you whether you should."

TikTok/Reels hooks:
1. "I built an app that tells me NOT to buy stuff."
2. "Before you spend $100, do this 30-second test."
3. "I wanted these $200 headphones. My app told me NO."
4. "POV: you have an impulse-buying problem."
5. "I made a bouncer for my bank account."

Video format:
- Show the product on screen.
- Enter price and answers.
- Reveal BUY / WAIT / DON'T BUY.
- Show cost per use.
- End with "Try it free — link in bio."

Pricing:
Free: 3 checks/month.
Pro: $3/month, unlimited checks + saved cloud history + future features.

Do not claim the tool gives financial advice. It is a spending decision aid.
